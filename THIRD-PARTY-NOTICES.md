# Third-party notices

Belay currently includes **no third-party code or text**. Its wording, structure and code
are original.

If small pieces of MIT or Apache-2.0 material are ever adapted, the file that holds them
gets an attribution header, keeps the original copyright and licence text, and is listed
here with its source link and what changed. Nothing is taken from projects without a
licence or under share-alike terms.
